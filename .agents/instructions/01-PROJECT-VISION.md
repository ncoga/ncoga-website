# NCOGA project vision

## Identity and scope

The National Council of Ghanaian Associations (NCOGA) is the parent organization. Its official site is [ncoga.com](https://ncoga.com/). NCOGA Young Adult & Youth (NCOGA YAY) is a branch within NCOGA, established to serve young adults and youth. Its experience needs a recognizable character within one connected organizational and technical system.

This project transforms a copied, functional website codebase into NCOGA's digital home. Reuse sound infrastructure and components where practical, while replacing the previous organization's identity and building an NCOGA-specific content architecture and design system. The long-term platform should support organizational information, community participation, news, events, programs, partnerships, membership exploration, and sustained publishing.

NCOGA's official mission and vision must come from verified organizational sources. The master prompt quotes a mission about empowering and unifying Ghanaians and the diaspora through heritage, development, and advocacy; verify its exact wording before publication. “Rooted in Ghana. Connected Across Borders.” and “Rooted in Ghana. United Across Borders.” are **proposed editorial directions**, not approved slogans or substitutes for the official mission.

## Audiences and experience

Serve Ghanaian associations and diaspora members, young adults and students, professionals, community leaders, partners, sponsors, volunteers, media, event participants, and people considering membership. Visitors should quickly understand what NCOGA does, how YAY relates to it, and which participation path fits them.

Aim for a site that is culturally Ghanaian, institutionally credible, contemporary, welcoming, distinctive, accessible, and maintainable. Use authentic people and stories, clear information hierarchy, editorial composition, and restrained cultural details. Avoid a tourism aesthetic, a generic nonprofit template, or a startup landing page.

## Core principles and success

- Establish and review a coherent design system before widespread page work; carry it through shared components and YAY variants.
- Make NCOGA and YAY distinct but connected in navigation, content, and visual identity.
- Verify organizational claims and preserve provenance. Never invent leaders, events, impact numbers, testimonials, or history.
- Preserve useful existing mechanics without preserving irrelevant previous branding or private data.
- Build a living publishing platform with accessible, responsive, secure, performant, and discoverable pages.
- Prefer progressive disclosure and real participation paths over decorative sections or fabricated activity.

The project's success is a sustainable foundation that future agents and editors can extend consistently, not simply a visually attractive homepage. See [workflow](02-AGENT-WORKFLOW.md), [design direction](03-DESIGN-DIRECTION.md), and [information architecture](08-INFORMATION-ARCHITECTURE.md).
