# Design and engineering references

These links are research inputs, not approved dependencies, patterns to copy, or evidence that research has been completed. For each useful example, record its URL, site type, what works, what does not fit NCOGA, the specific pattern, accessibility and performance implications, possible reinterpretation, and licensing. Findings belong in [DESIGN_RESEARCH.md](../../docs/design/DESIGN_RESEARCH.md).

| Resource                                              | What to study and when relevant                                      | Do not copy blindly                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |

| [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/)                 | Strong art direction and section composition during exploration      | Award-site effects that harm usability                          |
| [Godly](https://godly.website/)                       | Contemporary composition and visual pacing                           | Fashion-driven patterns without organizational fit              |

| [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/)                         | Proven interaction and UX patterns                                   | App patterns unsuited to an editorial site                      |
| [404s.design](https://404s.design/)                   | Creative interface ideas                                             | Novelty at the expense of clarity                               |

| [W3C WAI](https://www.w3.org/WAI/)                    | Authoritative accessibility guidance                                 | Skipping manual keyboard testing                                |

Research contemporary Ghanaian graphic design, architecture, publications, photography, cultural institutions, diaspora sites, Kente geometry, and Adinkra symbolism. Document a proposed symbol's meaning, cultural context, and intended usage. Avoid collapsing Ghana's diverse cultures into one motif.

For GitHub examples of React or Next.js sites, inspect maintenance activity, architecture, accessibility, design tokens, motion quality, responsive behavior, performance, and license before reusing code. Do not install Lenis, React Bits, Motion, GSAP, or any other tool merely because it appears in this list.
